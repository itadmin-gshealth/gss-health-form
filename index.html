Terms and Conditions, CHW Signature

  • One form per student. Complete and submit a separate form for each student with a chronic health condition. For example, if a school has four affected students, the form should be submitted four times. If a student has multiple health conditions, select all applicable conditions. Wherever the form refers to "the condition," please answer with respect to the most serious condition.
  • No cases? Please submit the form anyway. If your school has no students with chronic health conditions, enter "NIL" in the Student Name and Student Code fields, and submit the form. This confirms that the school has been screened and that no cases were identified. Such submissions are just as important as case reports.
  • Obtain parental or guardian consent. Before collecting any information, explain to the parent or guardian what data is being collected, why it is needed, and how it will be used. Confirm that they have given their consent.
  • The form includes a declaration to verify that consent has been obtained. Do not submit a student's information without the family's consent. Enter the Student Code accurately. Copy the student code exactly as provided. This unique identifier helps ensure that each student is recorded only once and prevents duplicate entries.
  • Review, sign, and submit. Before submitting the form, ensure that all required fields have been completed accurately, the information provided is correct, and all necessary conditions and declarations have been fulfilled. Only then should the form be signed and submitted.
